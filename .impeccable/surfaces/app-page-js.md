## Scope

The Diaphora Labs public site: home, Niagara, Delta 0, Delta 1, Delta 2, Project: Waterfall.

## Visitor mode

Persuade. The site is the product's first argument, read by four audiences at once.

## Audience and job

- Founders — decide whether Delta 0 is worth using today and whether Delta 1 is worth waiting for.
- Investors — judge whether the pipeline and position are real.
- Corporate and funding partners — Delta 1 is unfunded; this site is how the conversation starts.
- Government ministers and civic stakeholders — judge legitimacy and regional impact.

## Actions

- Delta 0: strike a mark, self-service, no gate. No conversion required; the page *is* the
  product demo. Two live off-site doors are linked from the page as a ruled register of
  the three pieces with their real states — community **https://discord.gg/AnWHBBTjB**
  (carries the one `--current` action), source **https://github.com/francois-build/delta-0**
  (public, MIT), wiki unlinked and marked "Not live yet". An unstruck row reads as pending;
  a dead link reads as broken.
- Delta 1: two conversions on one page, deliberately equal — founder waitlist and partner/funder contact.
- Delta 2: email capture against a blank die.

## Proof and content constraints

No photographs, no named partners, no alumni, no metrics, no press. Delta 1 is unfunded
and the site says so. Project: Waterfall is a stated intention with no lease, purchase or
approval, and the page says that too. Absence is designed for, never filled in.

## Chosen direction

Hallmark — see DESIGN.md. User-pinned across a bolder re-roll; sharpened by the user's
clockmaker note into the signed-dial / punched-movement split that now carries the
Delta 0 vs Delta 1 architecture.

## Memorable moment

The strike. On Delta 0 it is genuinely self-service: the visitor composes a mark from the
fifteen values, signs it, and the file downloads. Nothing is sent, recorded, or approved.

## Unresolved

Delta 1 cohort dates, size, length, terms and equity. The Delta 0 **wiki** URL and host
(the community and the source are settled — see Actions). Capture provider (currently local
JSONL via `/api/register`). Whether WCAG 2.1 AA is binding. Historical claims on the
Niagara page need factual verification before launch, and the page now names the economic
figures it deliberately does not carry: annual visitor volume, drive-time population and
business count, bridge crossing volumes, regional employment and sector mix, and the
station's condition and cost to occupy. None of those may be estimated.

## Project: Waterfall — coalition building

Six sign-on lanes (partners, design/engineering firms, startups/researchers, locals and
community, investors/anchor tenants, media), each with its own action and copy that
states plainly what does not exist yet. Commitments render as struck punches. The
community lane always shows an open "what should this building become?" field, and
explicitly welcomes objections. A supporter register sits at the foot of the page,
empty and saying so.

Adapted from an earlier proposal document for the same building. Its calls to action,
supporter wall, and community-input mechanisms were carried over; its claims — a
seventeen-range test catalogue, a "Niagara-Proven" mark, six named building uses, a
global design competition, a press kit — were not, and must not appear until real.
